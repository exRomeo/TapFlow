# TapFlow Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 10 November 2026_

Host this page at a public URL (for example GitHub Pages) and enter that URL in Play Console under **App content → Privacy policy**. Keep it in sync with the in-app copy in `app/src/main/kotlin/dev/tapflow/autoclicker/ui/PrivacyPolicy.kt`.

## Summary
TapFlow works entirely on your device. It has no account, no ads, no analytics and no internet access, so nothing you do in TapFlow is sent anywhere.

## Accessibility Service
TapFlow uses Android's Accessibility Service API only to perform the taps, long presses and swipes you set up, when you start a script. It does not read the text or content of other apps' windows and does not monitor what you do.

## Screenshots (image steps)
If you use image steps, TapFlow takes screenshots while a script with an image step is running, and once when you capture an image. Screenshots are compared with your images in memory on the device and then discarded. They are never saved or sent.

## What is stored
Your scripts (target positions, timings and settings) and the small images you cut out for image steps are saved in TapFlow's private storage on your device. Scripts may be included in your Android device backup; the images are not. Uninstalling TapFlow, or clearing its storage, deletes all of it.

## Sharing
TapFlow does not share, sell or transfer any data to anyone.

## Changes
If TapFlow ever starts using your data in a new way, it will ask for your agreement again first.
